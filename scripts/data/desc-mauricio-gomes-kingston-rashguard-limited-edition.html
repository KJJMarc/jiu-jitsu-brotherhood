Introducing the Limited Edition Mauricio Gomes Kingston Club Rashguard - a tribute to our proud affiliation to the Mauricio Gomes Legacy team.

Featuring a vibrant design showcasing both the Mauricio Gomes Legacy team emblem and Kingston Jiu Jitsu Club logo.

Our rashguards are made from heavy-duty Lycra to protect your skin during no-gi training and competition.

  • Can also be used under your kimono as well to help protect your skin.
  • Allows moisture to pass through rather than soaking up like a t-shirt.
  • Cut long to make it easy to tuck in. Flat stitching to prevent irritation.
  • Wider cut neck for less restriction.
  • Sublimated logos and patterns so they will not peel off in the dryer or while training.
