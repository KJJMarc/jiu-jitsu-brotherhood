A special collaboration celebrating the roots of Kingston Jiu Jitsu and the legacy of Professor Mauricio Gomes.

This limited edition gi features a custom inner lining with a striking sublimated artwork of Kingston Bridge at sunset. The illustration was created by artist Mark Stroud and captures one of the most recognisable landmarks in Kingston upon Thames. It reflects the place where our academy and community have grown.

The result is a gi that combines tradition, craftsmanship, and local identity.

 

Key features

  • Limited edition collaboration with Mauricio Gomes
  • Custom sublimated lining featuring Kingston Bridge artwork by Mark Stroud
  • Exclusive Kingston Jiu Jitsu design
  • Lightweight pearl weave jacket for durability and comfort
  • Reinforced stitching in all key stress areas
  • Competition-legal construction
  • Unique woven Kingston Jiu Jitsu detailing

 

Why this gi is special

Kingston Jiu Jitsu has always been about more than just training. It is about community, history, and connection to the place where we train.

The lining artwork celebrates Kingston Bridge and the River Thames, a scene familiar to anyone who has spent time in the town. It is a small but meaningful detail that makes this gi unmistakably ours.

Once these are gone, they will not be reproduced.
