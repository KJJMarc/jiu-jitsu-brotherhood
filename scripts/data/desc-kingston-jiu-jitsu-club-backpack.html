The Kingston Jiu Jitsu backpack is designed to be the ultimate kit bag for the jiu jitsu athlete, this versatile carryall will get your gear to the gym and back in style. 

Features:

  • Large central area
  • Mesh ventilation for breathability
  • Easily holds 2 Gis 
  • Moisture-resistant compartment for ice-packs etc.
  • Valuables compartment
  • Heavy-duty durable zippers
  • Can convert between back-pack and hold-all configurations
  • High-quality embroidered Kingston Jiu Jitsu logo
  • Approx. dimensions: 52cm x 45cm x 25cm
